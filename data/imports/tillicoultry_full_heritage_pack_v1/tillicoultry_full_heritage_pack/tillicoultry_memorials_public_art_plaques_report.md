# Tillicoultry Memorials, Public Art, Plaques and Inscriptions Report

Research date: 2026-07-29

Every retained entry has a date or evidence range. No entry was excluded for lack of a date.

## Tillicoultry First World War Memorial

- **Type:** War Memorial
- **Location:** Tillicoultry Cemetery
- **Date:** Unveiled 23 October 1921
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Contemplative female figure with wreath on a granite pedestal, commemorating 64 dead.

## Tillicoultry Second World War Garden of Remembrance

- **Type:** War Memorial Garden
- **Location:** Tillicoultry Cemetery
- **Date:** Unveiled and dedicated 7 November 1948
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Thirty-two individual commemorative stones arranged behind the First World War memorial.

## St Serf's Parish Church First World War Plaque

- **Type:** Wall Plaque
- **Location:** St Serf's Parish Church
- **Date:** Installed approximately 1919–1925
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Description:** Bronze roll-of-honour plaque in a walnut frame.

## St Serf's Parish Church Second World War Plaque

- **Type:** Wall Plaque
- **Location:** St Serf's Parish Church
- **Date:** Installed approximately 1945–1955
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Description:** Bronze plaque commemorating local Second World War dead.

## Evangelical Union Church Corner of Remembrance

- **Type:** Church Memorial
- **Location:** Evangelical Union Congregational Church; exact location to verify
- **Date:** Established approximately 1945–1955
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Description:** Corner of remembrance and roll-of-honour plaque for six members.

## Devonvale Hall Date Plaque

- **Type:** Architectural Date Plaque
- **Location:** Principal elevation of Devonvale Hall
- **Date:** Dated 1938
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Art Deco plaque reading 'DEVONVALE / 19 HALL 38' with butterfly motifs.

## Devonvale Hall Foundation-Stone Plaque

- **Type:** Foundation Stone Plaque
- **Location:** Right of the main entrance, Devonvale Hall
- **Date:** Installed during construction, 1938–1940
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Bronze plaque commemorating the laying of the foundation stone.

## Walker Fountain

- **Type:** Memorial Fountain
- **Location:** Central Tillicoultry; exact coordinate to verify
- **Date:** 1900
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Granite commemorative fountain identified in the conservation appraisal.

## Popular Institute Clock Tower Civic Gift

- **Type:** Commemorative Clock Tower
- **Location:** Ochil Street
- **Date:** Added in 1878
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Clock tower presented by mill owner James Archibald to the Popular Institute.

## War Memorial Conservation Works

- **Type:** Memorial Conservation Event
- **Location:** Tillicoultry Cemetery
- **Date:** Cleaning and conservation reported in 2016
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Description:** Modern conservation campaign affecting the principal war memorials.
