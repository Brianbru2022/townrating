# Alva Memorials, Public Art, Plaques and Inscriptions Report

Research date: 2026-07-29

## Scope

Separate inventory of war memorials, mausoleums, commemorative plaques, memorial fountains, military inscriptions and public sculpture associated with Alva. Every retained entry has a date or evidence range; none was excluded for lack of a date.

## Principal town memorials

### Alva War Memorial

- **Type:** War Memorial
- **Location:** Cochrane Park, Beauclerc Street
- **Date:** Unveiled and dedicated on 21 October 1923; Second World War additions unveiled in June 1951
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Celtic cross with bronze name plaques, winged cherub and Sword of Sacrifice; 138 First World War and 37 Second World War names.
- **Designer or maker:** William Kerr; bronze plaques by Charles Henshaw; carving by Holmes & Jackson
- **Reference:** IWM WMR 6029, War Memorials Online 151036

### Alva War Memorial 1998 refurbishment

- **Type:** Memorial Conservation Event
- **Location:** Cochrane Park, Beauclerc Street
- **Date:** Refurbished in spring 1998
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** £10,000 conservation and refurbishment programme.
- **Designer or maker:** Not established
- **Reference:** IWM WMR 6029

### Alva Parish Church First World War Memorial Plaque

- **Type:** Wall Plaque
- **Location:** Alva Cemetery waiting room; formerly Alva Parish Church
- **Date:** Unveiled and dedicated on 16 September 1923; moved to the cemetery waiting room around 1984–1985
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** White-marble wall tablet listing 109 First World War names, relocated after the church fire.
- **Designer or maker:** Messrs McGlashen & Stewart, Edinburgh
- **Reference:** No formal identifier recorded

### Alva-Johnstone Mausoleum

- **Type:** Mausoleum
- **Location:** Alva Churchyard
- **Date:** 1790
- **Confidence:** High
- **Description:** Nationally important Graeco-Roman family mausoleum.
- **Designer or maker:** Robert and James Adam
- **Reference:** HES LB21031

### Carrie Johnstone Memorial Fountain

- **Type:** Memorial Fountain
- **Location:** Johnstone / Cochrane Park, Alva
- **Date:** Erected in 1951 in memory of Carrie Johnstone, who died in 1929
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Description:** Memorial drinking fountain associated with the Johnstone family and public park.
- **Designer or maker:** Not established
- **Reference:** No formal identifier recorded

## Nearby public art

### River Spirit

- **Location:** Collylands Roundabout, between Sauchie and Alva; outside the Alva town core
- **Date:** Installed on 1 June 2007 according to contemporary reports; one later image source labels it June 2011
- **Artist:** Andy Scott
- **Scope:** Nearby approach-road public art, not inside the Alva historic core.

## Date interpretation

Memorial dates can refer to unveiling, dedication, death, relocation, addition, installation or conservation. The app should always show the full date wording.
