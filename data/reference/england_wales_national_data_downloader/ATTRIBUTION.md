# Attribution and licence notes

Always retain each dataset's title, provider, source URL, licence, retrieval date and native identifier in your ingestion database.

## Historic England

Most datasets downloaded from the Historic England Open Data Hub are supplied under the **Open Government Licence (OGL)**. Historic England services can also contain Ordnance Survey-derived geometry or background information, so preserve any attribution stated in the service metadata.

Suggested credit:

> Contains Historic England data licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Where stated in the source metadata, contains Ordnance Survey data © Crown copyright and database right.

## Cadw and DataMapWales

Cadw designation layers and many DataMapWales layers are published under the **Open Government Licence**, but confirm the licence shown on each current dataset page or WFS metadata record.

Suggested credit:

> Contains Cadw / Welsh Government data licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## RCAHMW / National Monuments Record of Wales

For the open NMRW terrestrial and maritime site datasets, retain the provider's specified attribution:

> This site data is from the National Monuments Record of Wales (NMRW), produced by the Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Wales (RCAHMW). © Crown Database Right: RCAHMW, licensed under the Open Government Licence 3.0.

Coflein photographs, scans, reports and archive media can have separate rights. Archwilio records and historic-place-name exports should be checked against their current terms before bulk republication.

## Ordnance Survey, ONS, Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales

Apply the licence and attribution specified for each supporting dataset. OS OpenData commonly requires OS attribution; ONS open geography commonly uses the OGL; Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales datasets can have dataset-specific terms.

Do not imply endorsement by Historic England, Cadw, Welsh Government, RCAHMW, any local authority or any other provider.
